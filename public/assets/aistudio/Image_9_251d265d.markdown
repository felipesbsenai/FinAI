## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, empathetic financial companion tailored for personal wealth management and AI-guided spending decisions. The aesthetic balances the institutional stability of modern neo-banking (clean lines, confident typography, structural restraint) with the fluid agility of productivity engines (micro-glows, tactile surface response, layered glass surfaces).

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Trustworthy & Prudent:** Every financial datum is rendered with clarity and structural balance; no ambiguous layouts or visual noise.
- **Intelligent & Proactive:** AI insights appear as contextual highlights, using refined luminescence and glassmorphism rather than intrusive conversational patterns.
- **Approachable Luxury:** Generous negative space, soft ambient illumination, and precise optical curvature evoke an effortless, premium digital banking experience.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **Modern Fintech Minimalism** and **Refined Glassmorphism**. Structural integrity is maintained via strict grid alignments and tonal depth, while floating contextual surfaces leverage backdrop blurs and luminous edge-highlights to distinguish analytical insights from core transaction ledgers.

## Colors

The palette employs an authoritative indigo core paired with an affirmative emerald accent to signify forward momentum, asset growth, and algorithmic clarity. Surfaces scale seamlessly across dual modes, operating with high-contrast functional accessibility compliant with WCAG AA standards.

### Core Semantic Spectrum
- **Primary (`#6366F1`):** Represents AI synthesis, automated categorization, and primary directional actions.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Reserved for positive cash flow, budget surpluses, investment gains, and verified balance states.
- **Alert / Warning (`#EF4444` / `#F59E0B`):** Explicitly marks overspending thresholds, bill maturity dates, and budget alerts.

### Transactional Category Tokens
- **Alimentação:** `#F97316` (Warm Tangerine)
- **Transporte:** `#3B82F6` (Direct Azure)
- **Educação:** `#8B5CF6` (Deep Amethyst)
- **Lazer:** `#EC4899` (Vibrant Rose)
- **Outros:** `#6B7280` (Neutral Slate)

### Theme Transitions
- **Dark Mode (Default):** Deep slate canvas (`#0B0F19`) overlaid with stacked surface containers (`#111827`, `#1E293B`), accented by subtle linear borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and directional back-glows (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`).
- **Light Mode:** Crisp off-white foundation (`#F8FAFC`) with pure white card planes (`#FFFFFF`), delimited by ultra-soft drop shadows and neutral division contours (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes scanning efficiency and numerical prominence. **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers geometry and character to high-level summaries and monetary values, while **Inter** guarantees neutral legibility across dense line items, metadata, and Brazilian Portuguese financial descriptors.

### Monetary Presentation (Locale pt-BR)
- The currency prefix (`R$`) must always be rendered with a lighter optical weight or smaller size than the integer values (e.g., `font-weight: 500` for `R$` vs `font-weight: 800` for digits) to prevent symbol intrusion.
- Fractional amounts (cents) maintain optical alignment; they should never break across lines.
- Always use standard Brazilian formatting (`.` for thousands separator, `,` for decimal values: `R$ 12.450,80`).
- Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for numeric data across transaction tables, balance displays, and telemetry charts.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural model operates on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight numeric alignments. The layout adapts between an application viewport (PWA shell) and full-scale desktop analytical dashboards.

### Responsive Architecture
- **Mobile PWA (< 640px):** Single-column layout with fixed bottom navigation and contextual floating actions. Canvas margin is locked to `1rem` (`16px`) to maximize surface utility for complex charts and financial lists.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with fluid card expansion. Side navigation appears as an icon-rail. Canvas margin increases to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max container (`1280px` max-width). Sidebar expands into full-hierarchy tree. Analytical dashboards split into asymmetrical modular widgets (7 columns balance & trends, 5 columns AI suggestions & budget status).

### Rhythm & Padding Rules
- Financial summary cards maintain `1.5rem` internal padding on desktop and `1.25rem` on mobile.
- Transaction line items use `0.75rem` vertical spacing to preserve continuous scroll efficiency while preventing mis-taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual plane separation avoids heavy opaque drop shadows, relying instead on structural layering, tonal surfaces, and luminous boundaries.

### Dark Mode Stratification
1. **Canvas Layer (`#0B0F19`):** Root background, strictly flat.
2. **Surface Layer (`#111827`):** Base panels and transaction blocks. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Elevated / Interactive Layer (`#1E293B`):** Modals, dynamic sheets, and active cards. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` accompanied by an ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
4. **AI Highlight / Focus State:** Surface inherits a radial gradient border coupled with an atmospheric violet glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.

### Light Mode Stratification
- Cards rest on a `#FFFFFF` baseline over `#F8FAFC`, delimited by a subtle dual-layer shadow:
  - Ambient: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - Key: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`
- Modals utilize an elevated float: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

### Glassmorphism Treatment
Applied selectively to navigation bars, floating quick-action docks, and AI status badges:
- Backdrop blur: `16px` to `20px`.
- Background opacity: 75% dark (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`), 85% light (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
- Border contour: 1px continuous top-highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` in dark, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` in light).

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses generous, friendly curvatures (16px–20px) to balance the analytical nature of financial data with modern digital warmth.

### Curvature Assignments
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Segmented Controls):** `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` (8px–12px) to ensure clear tap boundaries.
- **Card Enclosures & Modules:** `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px–20px), defining the visual signature of the interface.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Dialogs:** `1.5rem` (24px) top-radii for tactile pull-up sheets.
- **Status Pills, Category Tags, and Avatars:** Fully rounded / pill-shaped (`9999px`).

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary:** Violet foundation (`#6366F1`) with white text. High-contrast state features a subtle inner top-border highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Height: `44px` mobile, `40px` desktop.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a dynamic boundary (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` in dark; `1px solid #E2E8F0` in light).
- **Affirmative Actions (Quick Save / Invest):** Emerald base (`#10B981`) with hover elevation.

### Financial Summary Cards
- Core card radius fixed at `18px`.
- Displays total net balance, periodic burn rate, and AI health index.
- Includes a top-right contextual sparkline or miniature trend indicator (`+12,4% este mês`).
- Border contains a soft directional gradient: lighter at top-left, dissolving at bottom-right.

### Transaction Line Items
- Left: Category-colored rounded icon container (40x40px, `rounded-xl`, category background at 15% opacity with 100% solid category icon).
- Center: Double-line metadata stack (Title: `body-md` in high-emphasis neutral; Subtitle: time and account tag in `body-sm` muted).
- Right: Monetary balance right-aligned with signed values (`- R$ 84,20` in neutral emphasis, `+ R$ 2.400,00` in Secondary Emerald).

### Category Chips & Filters
- Compact height (`32px`), pill-shaped (`rounded-full`).
- Active state: Category solid tint or white outline with micro color indicator dot (6px).
- Inactive state: Low-emphasis background with subtle text contrast.

### Form Inputs & Monetary Capture
- Standard inputs: Floating label architecture with `12px` padding, smooth outline transition to `#6366F1` on focus.
- Monetary Input Field: Extra-large currency display (`display-currency`) centered or left-aligned, auto-scaling to text length with an anchored, low-opacity `R$` prefix.

### AI Contextual Insight Banners
- Encased in a high-blur translucent layer (`backdrop-blur-md`).
- Left-edge primary accent stroke (`3px solid #6366F1`).
- Typography pairs an AI sparkle glyph with concise recommendations in `body-sm`, accompanied by inline one-tap confirmations (`"Categorizar como Transporte?"`).
